# Industry universe

A starting deck for industry rounds. Wide on purpose: the right answer for someone who doesn't know yet is often in a sector she has never pictured. Each line: industry · what it does · who it hires from science. Treat named examples as unverified until scouting confirms them.

## Energy and climate
- **Battery materials and energy storage** · cells, cathode/anode materials, recycling · materials and electrochemistry people
- **Solar and photovoltaics** · panels, coatings, new cell types · thin-film and device people
- **Hydrogen and fuel cells** · electrolysers, catalysts, membranes · surface and electrochemistry people
- **Carbon capture and carbon accounting** · removing CO₂; measuring and verifying emissions · chemists, analysts, verifiers
- **Utilities and grid** · power companies and their labs, planning teams · analysts, technologists
- **Nuclear** · power plants, isotopes, waste management · chemists for plant chemistry and radiochemistry
- **Critical minerals and mining technology** · extracting and refining battery metals · process and analytical chemists

## Materials and making things
- **Specialty chemicals** · the ingredients inside everyday products · R&D, QC, technical service
- **Paints, coatings and adhesives** · surface chemistry at industrial scale · formulation chemists
- **Semiconductors and electronics materials** · chips, thin films, cleanroom processes · process and characterisation people
- **Advanced manufacturing and 3D printing** · new materials and how to print them · materials scientists
- **Glass, ceramics and optics** · lenses, displays, coatings · materials and optics people
- **Packaging and plastics (incl. sustainable packaging)** · barrier films, recyclable materials · polymer and surface chemists

## Health and life sciences
- **Pharmaceuticals (manufacturing and QC)** · making and testing medicines · QC, QA, analytical development
- **Medical devices and diagnostics** · tests, sensors, implants · R&D, verification, regulatory
- **Contract research and testing labs (CROs/CDMOs)** · labs that run tests for other companies · analysts, project coordinators
- **Biotech** · drugs and tools from biology · research associates, analytical support
- **Cosmetics and personal care** · formulation, claims testing, safety · formulation chemists, regulatory

## Environment and water
- **Water treatment and membranes** · cleaning and testing water · process and analytical chemists
- **Environmental consulting** · site assessment, remediation, impact studies · environmental scientists
- **Environmental testing labs** · measuring contaminants (PFAS, metals) · analysts
- **Waste, recycling and circular economy** · turning waste into materials · process chemists, analysts

## Food and agriculture
- **Food science and ingredients** · texture, flavour, shelf life, safety · food chemists, QA
- **Agricultural inputs and crop science** · fertilisers, crop protection · analytical and formulation chemists
- **Beverage, brewing and distilling** · quality and process labs · QC chemists

## Instruments and tools
- **Scientific instrument makers** · spectrometers, microscopes, sensors · application scientists, sales, service
- **Lab supplies and reagents** · the materials labs buy · technical sales, product specialists
- **Sensors and IoT hardware** · devices that measure the world · device and test scientists

## Public sector and research
- **National research labs** · government science (energy, materials, measurement standards) · technologists, research officers
- **Regulators** · agencies that approve chemicals, pesticides, drugs, devices · evaluators, regulatory scientists
- **Research funders and granting agencies** · decide what science gets funded · program officers, analysts
- **Universities (staff, not faculty)** · core facilities, tech transfer, research admin · facility technologists, coordinators
- **Policy and think tanks** · energy, climate, innovation policy · junior analysts

## Knowledge and services
- **Intellectual property (patent firms)** · protecting inventions · technical specialists, patent agent trainees
- **Consulting (technical, environmental, management)** · advising clients · analysts
- **Science publishing and journals** · editing and producing research · editorial assistants
- **Medical and science communications agencies** · writing for pharma and health · associate writers
- **Deep-tech venture capital and incubators** · funding and building science startups · analysts

## Wildcard pool (sectors people rarely picture)
- **Art conservation and heritage science** · analysing and preserving artworks and artefacts
- **Forensic science** · lab analysis for police and courts
- **Anti-doping and sports testing** · labs that test athletes' samples
- **Metrology and standards** · the organisations that define and certify measurements
- **Space and aerospace materials** · coatings and materials for spacecraft and aircraft
- **Perfume and flavour houses** · chemistry of smell and taste
- **Insurance and risk (technical)** · assessing chemical, environmental or product risk
- **Education technology and science learning products** · building lab kits, simulations and courses
- **Emergency management and hazmat** · chemical incident planning and response
- **Museums and science centres** · exhibits, programmes, outreach
