# Role universe

A starting deck for role rounds, deliberately wide. Grouped by the kind of work, because that's what reactions teach. Each line: role · what it is · typical entry title. Science-heavy roles come first because that's where most science grads start looking, but deal from every group. Research beyond this list whenever her background or signals point somewhere it doesn't cover.

## Making and discovering (hands-on lab or technical)
- **R&D scientist / research associate** · develops new products or materials in a company lab · Research Associate, Scientist I
- **Process / scale-up chemist** · turns a lab recipe into something a plant can make · Process Chemist, Process Engineer-in-training
- **Formulation scientist** · designs mixtures (paints, cosmetics, drugs, foods) that behave the right way · Formulation Chemist
- **Materials characterisation specialist** · runs the instruments that tell you what something is made of · Characterisation Scientist, Lab Specialist
- **Device / prototype engineer-scientist** · builds and tests early hardware (batteries, sensors, solar) · R&D Technologist
- **Government research technologist** · lab work at a national or provincial research centre · Research Technologist, Research Officer (junior)
- **Lab manager (small lab or startup)** · keeps a lab running: instruments, safety, ordering, people · Lab Coordinator

## Checking and assuring
- **QC / QA analyst** · tests products against specifications; keeps the records that prove it · QC Chemist, QA Associate
- **Analytical chemist (contract lab)** · runs tests for many clients: water, soil, food, pharma · Analyst, Laboratory Technologist
- **Environmental scientist / technician** · samples and assesses sites, water and air · Environmental Technician, Junior Environmental Scientist
- **Method development / validation scientist** · designs and proves new ways to measure things · Associate Scientist, Analytical Development
- **Metrology / calibration specialist** · keeps measurements trustworthy · Calibration Technician
- **Forensic or anti-doping lab analyst** · analysis where results go to court or to sport bodies · Forensic Analyst (entry)

## Explaining and translating
- **Application scientist** · shows customers how an instrument or material solves their problem · Applications Specialist
- **Technical / scientific writer** · writes manuals, reports, standard procedures, grant text · Technical Writer
- **Medical / science communications** · turns research into material for doctors, the public or investors · Associate Medical Writer
- **Science communicator / outreach** · museums, science centres, media, university outreach · Program Coordinator, Science Educator
- **Technical trainer** · teaches customers or staff to use complex equipment or software · Training Specialist
- **College or lab instructor** · teaches labs and courses · Lab Instructor, Teaching Technologist

## Persuading and selling
- **Technical sales representative** · sells instruments, chemicals or lab supplies to scientists · Inside Sales, Account Representative
- **Field application / customer success scientist** · keeps customers succeeding after the sale · Customer Success Scientist
- **Product manager (scientific products)** · decides what a product should do and why · Associate Product Manager
- **Business development (deep tech)** · finds partners and customers for a technology company · BD Associate

## Advising and deciding
- **Regulatory affairs specialist** · gets products approved and keeps them compliant · Regulatory Affairs Associate
- **Patent agent trainee / technical specialist** · helps protect inventions; judges what's new · Technical Specialist, Patent Agent Trainee
- **Science / energy policy analyst** · advises government on technology, energy, environment · Junior Policy Analyst
- **Grants / research program officer** · runs funding programmes at funders and agencies · Program Officer
- **Technology transfer associate** · helps a university turn research into companies and licences · Tech Transfer Associate
- **Technical consultant** · advises clients on engineering, environmental or energy questions · Consultant, Analyst
- **Management / strategy consultant** · solves business problems for clients; many hire science grads · Business Analyst
- **Deep-tech investment analyst** · evaluates science startups for VCs and funders · Analyst
- **Health and safety / EHS specialist** · keeps workplaces and chemical handling safe · EHS Coordinator

## Analysing and building with data
- **Lab data / scientific data analyst** · makes sense of instrument and experiment data · Data Analyst
- **Computational / simulation scientist** · models materials or processes on a computer · Junior Scientist (computational)
- **LIMS / lab software specialist** · configures the software labs run on · LIMS Analyst

## Organising and operating
- **Project coordinator (R&D or engineering)** · keeps complex projects on schedule · Project Coordinator
- **Supply chain / materials sourcing (technical)** · buys specialised materials and judges suppliers · Technical Buyer
- **Manufacturing / production supervisor-in-training** · runs a shift of a production line · Production Associate
- **Clinical research coordinator** · runs the day-to-day of clinical trials · Clinical Research Coordinator

## Starting something
- **Startup generalist** · early employee at a small science company, doing a bit of everything · Associate, Founding Scientist
- **Venture studio / incubator analyst** · helps build new companies from scratch · Analyst
